Privacy Policy — Räkna på svenska

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Räkna på svenska is an app for learning to count in Swedish: numbers, ordinals, the clock and fractions. It has no account, no login, no analytics and no server. This policy describes everything it does with data, which is very little.

What stays on your device

Two things are stored, only on your phone:

  • Your settings: speech speed, which Swedish voice to use, and the two learning options
  • Whether you have unlocked full access

None of this is sent anywhere, and none of it is readable by us. Uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no backup and no sync; if you change phone, use Restore a purchase in Settings to unlock full access again.

What leaves your device

The app itself sends nothing. Every number, ordinal, time and fraction is worked out on the phone.

Speech

The Swedish you hear is spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech engine, not by this app. Most voices work offline and send nothing. Some voices, marked Needs internet in Settings, are provided by your phone's speech engine over the network: when you use one, that engine sends the words being spoken — Swedish numbers such as trehundrafyrtiosju — to its provider, usually Google. That is governed by the provider's own privacy policy. Choose a voice marked Offline if you want nothing to leave the phone.

Purchases

Unlocking full access is handled entirely by Google Play. The purchase sheet, the payment and the receipt are Google's; we never see your payment details, your name or your account. The app learns only whether full access is owned, and stores that fact on your device.

If you write to us

The feedback button in Settings opens your own email app with a draft. Nothing is sent from inside the app: you write it, you can change or delete any part of it first — including the last lines, which name the app version and your phone's operating system — and you send it yourself from your own account.

If you do send it, we have your email address and whatever you wrote, because that is what email is. We use it to answer you and for nothing else, we do not add you to any list, and we never pass it on. Ask us to delete the exchange and we will.

What we never collect

The app collects no name, no email address, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no advertising identifier, no device identifier, no usage analytics and no crash reports. There are no advertising or analytics libraries in it. The one exception is the one you choose: if you email us, we have what you sent, as described above.

Children

The app collects nothing that could identify anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no data about you to look up, export or delete. The single exception is an email you sent us, which we can find and will delete on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version appears at this address.
